# Interpret LCA Results and Hotspots

## Core method

Interpret only accepted calculations within their declared boundary.

1. Verify result, functional-unit, method, category, unit, system-boundary, and version references.
2. Decompose contributions using deterministic results and a stated denominator.
3. Distinguish dominant contribution, sensitivity, uncertainty, data-quality weakness, and causal explanation.
4. Check whether exclusions, uncharacterized flows, allocation, or missing Providers could change the ranking.
5. State decision relevance, limitations, and the strongest defensible conclusion.

## Acceptance shape

Produce `result_interpretation` with ranked supported contributions, calculation references, robustness checks, and claim ceiling. A hotspot is a contribution pattern, not proof of a causal intervention effect.

## Common mistakes

- Comparing contributions calculated under different functional units or methods.
- Presenting rank as statistical or causal certainty.
- Ignoring missing inventory coverage.
- Hiding a bounded result behind polished recommendations.
