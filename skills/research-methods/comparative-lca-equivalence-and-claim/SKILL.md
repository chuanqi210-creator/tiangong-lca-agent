# Assess Comparative LCA Equivalence and Claims

## Core method

Do not compare until the bases are demonstrably equivalent or explicitly adjusted.

1. Align intended use, function, functional unit, reference flow, system boundary, lifecycle stages, geography, time, technology, allocation, cutoff, method, and indicator units.
2. Record every difference as equivalent, adjusted with a source-bound transformation, sensitivity-tested, or disqualifying.
3. Compare accepted results only after the equivalence ledger passes.
4. Evaluate uncertainty, completeness, and material assumptions before ranking.
5. Limit public claims to the evidence and review conditions actually satisfied.

## Acceptance shape

Produce `comparative_claim_assessment` containing an equivalence ledger, comparable result references, uncertainty/coverage limits, and an allowed claim or a no-comparison boundary.

## Common mistakes

- Using the same product name as proof of functional equivalence.
- Normalizing results without recording the transformation.
- Ranking alternatives when uncertainty or missing coverage can reverse the order.
- Turning an internal scenario comparison into a public superiority claim.
